  Gaps against the PDF

  This is from a quick scan of the company page and research cards, not a full
  audit.

  1. Balance sheet section (missing). The PDF lists debt, leverage,
     working-capital cycle and funding structure. Debt and working capital only
     appear as inputs to bull vs bear; there is no section of its own. The
     numbers are already pulled from screener.in, so this is the cheapest gap to
     close.
  2. Financial snapshot (missing as a block). There is no "at a glance" strip at
     the top of the company page with revenue, margin, PAT, ROCE and
     debt/equity.
  3. Trajectory is thin. It is one quarterly revenue chart. The PDF asks for
     "what's growing, what's compressing", so a multi-year view of revenue,
     margin and PAT with a one-line read would fit.
  4. Guidance is folded into the promise ledger. A "Guidance" filter or tab
     showing only growth, margin and capex guidance would make it visible as its
     own item on the menu.
  5. Cost and efficiency is invisible. The PDF gives bonus points for it. The
     usage endpoint exists, but nothing shows it in the app, and the write-up
     still has [TO FILL] for cost, cache hit rate, deployed link and repo link.
  6. README is stale. The deployed link is blank, and the Cron step still
     describes the old Render setup.

  Ideas from other apps

  Indian apps like Trendlyne and StockEdge lead with a few signals FINN does not
  have yet:

  - Insider and bulk/block deals: promoter buying or selling in a portfolio
    stock is a strong Push item and fits the urgent tier.
  - Shareholding pattern changes: a quarterly change in promoter, FII and DII
    holding, shown as one line on the company page.
  - Promoter pledge alert: the search found neither app advertising it, so it
    would be a differentiator and a natural tripwire.
  - "Why did it move" link: on a price alert, show any filing from the same day
    next to the move.
  - Home-screen summary line: one sentence above the brief, such as "3 things
    need you, 41 hidden".
